CLOCK-OUT BATTLE · PLAYER CARE

Privacy Policy

Your game data, and your choices.

Updated 30 October 2026

1. About this policy

This policy describes the offline Clock-Out Battle game, published by Shanghai Dayi Data Technology Co.,Ltd, and the support provided through lvanzc16@iwgame.com. It applies to the current free version of the game.

2. Information handled by the game

The game does not require an account and does not send gameplay records or personal information to a developer-operated server. It has no advertising, analytics, tracking SDKs, in-app purchases, or online multiplayer.

Level progress, earned tickets, language, and sound settings are stored locally on your device. We do not receive these records or share them with third parties. Your operating system may include app data in backups according to your settings.

3. Device permissions

The game does not request access to your location, contacts, photos, camera, or microphone.

4. When you contact support

If you email us, we receive your email address, message, and any attachments or device details you choose to send. We use that information to respond to and investigate your request. The game does not automatically send support messages or device diagnostics.

Support correspondence is handled through email service providers and may be retained as needed to resolve and document your request or meet applicable legal obligations. We do not use it for advertising or sell it. Please send only information relevant to your request.

5. Retention, deletion, and your choices

Local game data stays on your device until you remove it or the operating system clears it. Deleting the app and its data removes the local save; reinstalling may restore a backup if your device is configured to do so. Manage backups through your device settings.

To request access to, correction of, or deletion of information you sent to support, email lvanzc16@iwgame.com. You can also ask us to stop further optional correspondence. We will handle your request subject to any records we must keep under applicable law. There is no game account to delete.

6. This support website

These pages do not include advertising, analytics scripts, or a contact form. You contact us through your email application. The hosting service may process technical request information, such as an IP address and browser details, to deliver and protect the website. Such website requests are separate from the offline game and its local save data.

7. Updates and contact

We will update this page if the game or our privacy practices change. The date below identifies the current policy. For privacy questions, contact lvanzc16@iwgame.com.
