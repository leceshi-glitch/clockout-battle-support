How do I play?

Tap an arrow box whose path is clear. It moves into an available slot and automatically clears task balls of the matching color. Plan around the arrow directions and the number of free slots.

Do I need an account or internet connection?

No account or login is required. The game runs offline after installation. It has no advertisements, in-app purchases, or online multiplayer. Sending a support email requires an internet connection.

How do unlocks and tickets work?

Tap an available unlock or power option directly, without watching an advertisement or making a payment. Tickets are earned in the game and cannot be bought with real money. Each run has one shared revival opportunity, using the free option or 100 earned tickets.

Pause, sound, and language

Tap Pause during a level, then Resume to continue. Open Settings to change the sound or switch between English and Simplified Chinese. The game is designed for landscape play and touch controls.

Where is my progress saved?

Progress, earned tickets, and settings are saved on your device. The game does not provide account-based cloud saves or cross-device synchronization. Deleting the app may remove your progress. A device backup may restore it depending on your operating system settings.

Something is not working

Close and reopen the game, and check for an available update. If the issue continues, email us with the details listed above. Avoid deleting the app while troubleshooting if you want to keep your local progress.
